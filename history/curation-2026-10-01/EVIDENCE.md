## Exact certificate meaning

A check of the customer NE equations and each **actual** unilateral first-stage deviation proves the reported approximation factor for that single input, provided the remaining labeled continuations are completed with an exact NE. The prescribed deterministic pure default supplies such a completion. The phi command-line verifier calls the same checking function as the constructor and ignores arbitrary text in its default-continuation metadata; it does not independently verify a supplied full off-path table. See [USAGE.md](../../USAGE.md) and [MODEL.md](MODEL.md).

The asym verifier's prior illegal-layout acceptance and the DP verifier's truncated-vector acceptance were corrected and regression checked in commit 70026ad. Internal adversarial agent reviews found no fatal theorem counterexample, but no unrelated external proof review is recorded.
