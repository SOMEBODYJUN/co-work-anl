# Actual-prefix harmonic kernel: independent check and exact earlier source

2026-10-25, credible11. Geometry11 requested independent examination of

\[
mW\ge\sum_x d_x a_m(c_x),\qquad
a_m(c)=\frac{c+1}{m}+H_{m-1}-H_c,\quad0\le c\le m.
\tag{1}
\]

Here `d_x` is membership multiplicity in any m legal comparison topics,
`c_x` is actual final load, and `H_0=0`. The original arbitrary catalog,
arbitrary player count, all complete-history pure SPE quantifiers apply.
The formula is valid. It is also exactly an existing kernel, rather than
a new all-input relationship from this round.

## Independent reconstruction

At actual position i let `r_i=m-i+1`, and let `b_i(x)` be the actual
preceding load. Against any forced legal topic A, all true future actions
can add at most `r_i-1` more coverers. Thus original SPE IC gives

\[
u_i\ge\sum_{x\in A}\frac1{b_i(x)+r_i}.
\]

Average the m indexed comparison topics, retaining repetitions, and sum
over actual roles. This gives

\[
mW\ge\sum_xd_x\sum_{i=1}^m\frac1{b_i(x)+m-i+1}.
\tag{2}
\]

Since `b_i(x)<=min(i-1,c_x)`, the inner sum is bounded below by its value
when all `c_x` actual appearances occur first. For `0<=c<=m-1` this value
is

\[
\frac{c+1}{m}+\sum_{s=c+1}^{m-1}\frac1s
=\frac{c+1}{m}+H_{m-1}-H_c.
\]

For `c=m`, every denominator is m, giving exactly one. Formula (1) also
gives one because `H_m-H_{m-1}=1/m`. This endpoint should be handled
separately in the direct counting proof, since there are only m roles,
not c+1=m+1 first roles. No shared simultaneous realization of the
clientwise worst orders is needed.

Equivalently,

\[
a_m(c)=\frac c m+H_m-H_c.
\tag{3}
\]

The endpoints are `a_m(0)=H_m`, `a_m(m-1)=a_m(m)=1`, and the coefficient
is nonincreasing in c, since
`a_m(c+1)-a_m(c)=1/m-1/(c+1)<=0`.

## Query-density caution relevant to bank closure

For the peer's full insurance family, uniform querying over its m(m-1)
Cartesian actions gives

\[
\rho(e_a)=\frac1m,\qquad
\rho(B_b)=\frac1{m-1}.
\]

Both tend to zero. Querying uniformly over any optimal m-slot tuple gives
each economic label once, and each bank once except one bank twice; hence
bank density is at most `2/m`. Thus its original individual-floor failure
already lies entirely in low query densities in an absolute asymptotic
sense. Low rho alone cannot be identified with private seats. A proposed
general dense/sparse decomposition must retain the joint incidence or
actual-load information that distinguishes fully covered bank customers
from omitted economic customers. This is a diagnostic of that proposed
all-input decomposition, not a new counterexample or restricted project.
