## 1. 探针实际做什么

源程序 `security/dual/probe_path_menu.py`（本批的脚本源另由相邻清洗批处理）先用稀疏整数客户质量生成一张完整有序策略：对每个深度从后向前计算每个动作沿真实后继的收益，按最大收益选动作并保留平局破法。随后 `build` 为每个有序历史和每个非规定动作建立真实偏离差行 `Gamma`，再沿实际路径收集菜单背景。`full=False` 时每个背景是强制当前动作后真实完整终局减去该动作单位向量，故为 n−1 个竞争槽；`full=True` 时不减去该向量，背景保留 n 个槽。重复动作、离轨历史和所有有序前缀仍以记录的 action map 保留，未被计数状态合并。

每次外层循环先在 `sum(w)=1`、`w≥0`、`Gamma w≥0` 下，以当前查询 `p` 的最小背景收益 `v` 为目标做浮点 LP；再固定得到的 `w`，在概率单纯形上用有限背景菜单最大化 `cap=min_B beta_B(p)·w`。输出 `W=c·w`（根策略覆盖质量）、`cap` 和 `gap=n·cap−W`。因此输出与 `W=1` 的固定策略安全锥探针、真实总福利归一化或额外槽目标都不是同一 LP；它不能直接替换相邻对象的量词。

所有源 JSON 的 `iteration` 均为 0；`weights` 只列大于阈值的非空兴趣掩码，`actions` 保留全有序策略动作。浮点 `Gamma` 没有在源中转 Fraction 或调用规范单位客户验证器，故候选点不能称完整纯 SPE 证书。

## 3. Mathematical status and comparison

This is finite discovery evidence for three generated policies. It differs from the existing full-policy alternating probe, which fixes a 13-player policy and normalizes total quality differently, and from the fixed three-player price certificates, which provide exact all-query duals for another fixed strategy. It also differs from the extra-slot aggregate probe: that source uses a separate random generator and objective. The present records do not supply a positive rational point, a complete exact `Gamma` certificate, or a proof that any displayed policy remains SPE after replacing the generator's weight by the LP output.

Accordingly, the records are retained as finite computations. They can guide a later exact replay of the three policy maps and path backgrounds, but they neither prove a general (n)-player inequality nor refute a fixed-policy or full-model bridge. In particular, they do not settle the six-provider Q-CAG-HALF target, and no numeric zero/negative gap should be read as a theorem about all complete histories, all policies, or all legal directories.

The mathematical object is intentionally linked to the adjacent fixed-policy cone and menu resources; no cross-model identity or phase-three hyperedge is asserted here.
