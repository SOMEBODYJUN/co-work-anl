固定列的AMOUNT最大输出是

\[
C_\ell=\sum_{j<\ell}p_j(B)
\max_{\alpha:j<i_\alpha\le\ell}\frac{q_{\alpha j\ell}}{p_j(W^\alpha)}.
\]
