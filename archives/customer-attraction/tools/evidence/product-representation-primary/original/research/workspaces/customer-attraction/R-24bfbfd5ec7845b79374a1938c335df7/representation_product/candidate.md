# Candidate use of the product representation

Unproved direction, separate from the exact product lemmas: determine whether arbitrary full-history SPE policies in a Cartesian product menu satisfy a global compensation budget inherited from their components, despite cross-coordinate continuation choices. P1 in `product_representation.md` proves only that componentwise products are complete SPE; it proves no decomposition for an arbitrary product SPE.

The exact envelope convolution P3 shows that a componentwise product cannot amplify a violation from policies that each satisfy the minimum-player price budget. A productive new mechanism would have to control or exploit coupled choices across components. No such control is claimed. General Q-CAG-HALF remains OPEN.
