# Direct global funding attempt: exact status and remaining obligation

2026-10-24, netglobal10. Original model and quantifiers are unchanged: arbitrary
number of unit providers, arbitrary finite nonempty common catalog, unit
customers, and every complete ordered-history pure SPE with arbitrary ties,
empty themes, repetitions, and zero input allowed. **Q-CAG-HALF remains OPEN.**

## 1. Inputs treated as established

The [round9 general handoff](../../round9/GENERAL_PROOF_WORKING.md),
[full-future coverer family](../../round9/parent/all_future_coverer_payment_family.md),
and [round8 joint primal](../../round8/residual/linear_joint_payment_certificate.md)
were read. The legal input interface is the enlarged one: source-preserving
customer density at most one, variable full-O ownership, all actual-reset
future coverer edges with the original per-edge cap, NET current clipping,
and whole-row cancellation of existing edges within the same query refund.
None of those facts is relabeled as fresh funding here.

The unresolved amount is the sum of requests associated with different
prescribed legal actions at the same actual role. For a fixed action, the
known grouping uses one actual-prefix whole query. Different actions only
give separate inequalities D_i(a)<=u_i. They do not give

    sum_a (requested amount for action a) <= u_i

unless a genuine common coefficient or another source of money is proved.
The distinct requests can concern disjoint source customers, so customer
inventory at most one does not itself bound the sum of whole-query
coefficients. This is a diagnosis of the missing derivation, not a claim that
ROLE is false for SPE.

## 2. Attempted general mechanisms and their precise stops

One attempted route was to carry full legal-theme packets rather than only
individual customers and choose routing so that the total whole-query
coefficient entering each actual role is at most one. The legal source
constraints bound each client's density and each origin's conserved mass.
They supply no proved bound on the sum, over distinct legal actions, of the
maximal density needed to dominate that action's requested vector. No
source-level exchange establishing that additional bound was found. It is
therefore not stated as a lemma or as an implicit consequence of f<=1.

A direct induction attempt retained the union of an arbitrary old prefix
rather than identifying remaining-player profit with total coverage. Two
possible targets were

    final total coverage >= OPT_r/2,
    OPT_r <= old-prefix coverage + 2*(remaining-player total profit).

Here OPT_r is a fresh r-theme optimum in the original catalog, and the old
prefix is included in final coverage in the first target. Neither assertion
was proved or used. The old-background pushforward counterexamples do not
automatically refute these assertions, since they concern different profit
objects; conversely, preserving that distinction creates no induction
credit. The missing insertion/current payment remains a genuine
cross-branch amount obligation. Tree10 independently exposed the same issue
through its exact active-profit insertion/deletion debit, without claiming
that the debit had been paid.

A third tempting simplification was to replace the mixed static safety
value by the best pure egalitarian portfolio and conjecture that actual
welfare pays m times its minimum provider utility. That exact candidate is
false for an explicit complete-SPE parameter family; see the
[separate proof](pure_egalitarian_detour_counterfamily.md). This alternative
was abandoned rather than weakened repeatedly. The family is not used as a
new primary research direction.

## 3. Constant price and five-player pressure check

For every point of the enlarged non-ROLE payment family, source mass
conservation still gives

    sum_i F_i = |O|.

Consequently the lambda_i=1 separation inequality is exactly

    |O| <= 2*sum_i u_i = 2W.

For optimal O this is the original target, not an independently funded dual
constraint. Neither packet language, alternate induction, nor the failed
pure-egalitarian bridge changed this fact.

Substituting m=5 into the established interface preserves OWN, legal
source/client density, one complete query per context, ALL-FUTURE/NET/ROW,
MASS, and the implication ROLE-feasible => OPT_5<=2W. This branch obtained
no unconditional cross-query inequality that makes ROLE feasible for every
five-player input. The parameter-family substitution in the separate note
is an exact diagnostic of its general bridge, not evidence for arbitrary
five-player HALF.

## 4. Honest handoff

No new all-input off-history IC funding relation and no genuine SPE
separation of the enlarged sufficient certificate were obtained. No new
instance or policy scan, fixed-packet replay, LP variant, local kernel, or
five-player coefficient adjustment was run. Failure to find a relation is
not evidence that such a relation, the enlarged certificate, or general
HALF is impossible.

The next effective proof step still has to use true off-history IC to
control several different legal-action requests jointly, with the same
initial d/H/J and actual-role wallets, or bypass the sufficient payment
interface with an actual all-input welfare proof. Merely restating that
joint inequality does not supply the money.
