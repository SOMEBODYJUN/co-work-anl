# Independent parent audit of the zero-regret background deletion witness

2026-10-24. Definition-level independent replay, separate from the author's search/simplification and audit code. This checks only a specified general deletion-repair interface, not Q-CAG-HALF. Internal review only; novelty and external review unverified.

The eight customer types in `../new_cross_branch/zero_regret_repair_witness.json` expand to exactly 18 different unit customers. `audit_zero_repair.py` independently converts those types to masks and checks the author's simplified integer-weight representation, then evaluates all four last-player replies at each possible first action using `Fraction`. It enumerates every pure last-player best-reply policy and every first-player best reply in both fixed-background games; results and raw hashes are in `zero_repair_independent_audit.json`.

With one fixed A incumbent, the original two-tail complete pure SPE has first C and last replies `(C,C,B,C)` after first `(A,B,C,D)`. The original first-tail payoffs by possible first action are `(25/6,11/2,15/2,31/6)`, so C is the unique prescribed best action, and all four last replies are best replies. The three-role terminal union covers all 18 clients. Removing only the A background load while retaining the complete two-tail strategy yields actual C,B with 17 covered customers. Its first-player forced menu under that **retained** strategy is `(8,17/2,17/2,8)`, so C has zero regret; the actual last action B is also a best reply. This proves total actual-node regret zero without calling the retained off-path policy credible.

In the deleted two-player game, the last-player best-reply menu is uniquely `(C,D,B,B)` after first `(A,B,C,D)`. Enumerating that sole full contingent last policy, first D is the unique best action with payoff 9 and terminal D,B covers 16 clients. Therefore every exact pure SPE after deletion has welfare 16, strictly below the retained plan's 17. Replicating each customer `t` times preserves all best-reply comparisons and zero retained actual-node regret while multiplying the welfare repair loss by t. No absolute loss budget depending only on the sum of actual-node deletion regrets can hold in the class of arbitrary legal backgrounds.

The original fixed A incumbent is not asserted to be an actual strategic root choice. The result does not refute a repair theorem using additional original-root incentive restrictions or the signed refund structure, and it is not a half counterexample. I checked the algebraic comparisons and the raw enumeration; no general canonical asset or external result is claimed here.
