# Minimum-payoff tail normalization: rejected candidate

2026-10-21. Candidate only, not a theorem. In CAG-MODEL one might try to prove that every instance has a minimum-welfare complete pure SPE in which a globally minimum-payoff player occurs last or penultimate. The endpoint fractional-price bounds would then imply half coverage.

The accompanying `tail_minimum_obstruction.md` refutes this candidate. Even allowing the complete strategy to change and allowing any reordering of the terminal actions does not repair it without a positive welfare increase. The 36-customer example has exact minimum SPE welfare 34 and exact minimum welfare among SPE paths with a global payoff minimum in the last two positions 36.

A softer relocation would need to pay this welfare increase. No compensated relocation theorem is claimed here.
