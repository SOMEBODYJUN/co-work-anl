# Covariance form of the root-reset budget

**Scope.** This is an exact identity at one arbitrary ordered history of one fixed complete-history pure SPE. It applies to every finite common catalog, every number `r>=2` of providers remaining (including the current provider), every legal prefix, and every distribution `p` on the catalog. It is a diagnostic/payment criterion, not a Q-CAG-HALF proof.

Fix the prefix and write `b_x` for the number of earlier providers covering customer `x`. For each possible current action `A`, force the current provider to choose `A` and then follow the same complete strategy at the resulting ordered history; call the resulting terminal `Z(A)`. Let `n_A(x)` be the number of the remaining `r-1` providers covering `x` in that terminal. Put
\[
 c_x=b_x+1,
 \qquad a_A(x)=\mathbf1_{x\in A},
 \qquad F_A(x)=\frac{n_A(x)}{c_x+n_A(x)}.
\]
Thus `F_A(x)` is the total final share of the later providers on `x` in branch `A`; the forced current provider, when it covers `x`, is included in the denominator `c_x+n_A(x)`.

The forced-current payoff and its SPE slack are
\[
 D(A)=\sum_x\frac{a_A(x)}{c_x+n_A(x)},
 \qquad e(A)=u-D(A)\ge0,
\]
where `u` is the prescribed current payoff at the prefix. For two actions `A,T`, define the extra-slot query payoff
\[
 K(T,A)=\sum_x\frac{a_T(x)}{c_x+a_A(x)+n_A(x)}.
\]
The signed root-reset term and common-customer tax are
\[
 R(A,T)=\sum_x
\frac{(a_A(x)-a_T(x))(n_A(x)-n_T(x))}
 {(c_x+n_A(x))(c_x+n_T(x))},
\]
\[
 J(A,T)=\sum_{x:a_A(x)=a_T(x)=1}
 \left[\frac1{(c_x+n_A(x))(c_x+n_A(x)+1)}
      +\frac1{(c_x+n_T(x))(c_x+n_T(x)+1)}\right].
\]
All denominators are positive because `c_x>=1`.

## Pair identity

A four-case check (`x` in neither, only `A`, only `T`, or both) gives
\[
 K(T,A)+K(A,T)-D(A)-D(T)=R(A,T)-J(A,T).
\tag{1}
\]
Since `e(A)=u-D(A)`, this is equivalently
\[
2u-K(T,A)-K(A,T)=e(A)+e(T)+J(A,T)-R(A,T).
\tag{2}
\]
The positive reset term is the only signed part. For `x\in A\setminus T`,
\[
\frac{n_A-n_T}{(c+n_A)(c+n_T)}
=\frac{F_A(x)-F_T(x)}{c};
\]
for `x\in T\setminus A` the analogous term is `(F_T-F_A)/c`. Hence
\[
R(A,T)=\sum_{x\in A\setminus T}\frac{F_A(x)-F_T(x)}{c_x}
      +\sum_{x\in T\setminus A}\frac{F_T(x)-F_A(x)}{c_x}.
\tag{3}
\]
This identifies `R` as a membership/downstream-tail-share covariance, discounted by old load; it is not an arbitrary algebraic error.

## i.i.d. source/query specialization

Let `A,T` be independent draws from the same distribution `p`. Write
\[
 p_x=\Pr_p[a_A(x)=1].
\]
Because `R(T,A)=R(A,T)` and the two draws are exchangeable, (3) gives the exact covariance formula
\[
\mathbb E_p R(A,T)
=2\sum_x\frac{\operatorname{Cov}_p(a_A(x),F_A(x))}{c_x}.
\tag{4}
\]
The common tax has expectation
\[
\mathbb E_pJ(A,T)
=2\sum_x p_x^2\,\mathbb E_p\left[
 \frac{a_A(x)}{(c_x+n_A(x))(c_x+n_A(x)+1)}\right].
\tag{5}
\]
Let
\[
 \bar D=\mathbb E_pD(A),\qquad
 \bar K=\mathbb E_{A,T\sim p}K(T,A),\qquad
 \bar e=\mathbb E_pe(A).
\]
Averaging (2) and using exchangeability yields
\[
\boxed{u-\bar K=\bar e+\tfrac12\mathbb E_pJ(A,T)-\tfrac12\mathbb E_pR(A,T).}
\tag{6}
\]
Equivalently, the same-distribution diagonal push `\bar K<=u` is valid **if and only if**
\[
\mathbb E_pR(A,T)\le 2\bar e+\mathbb E_pJ(A,T).
\tag{7}
\]
Thus the precise missing payment is the positive covariance of a current action's membership with its induced downstream coverage. Requiring pairwise `R<=e(A)+e(T)+J` is sufficient but stronger than (7); it is not necessary.

The identities remain valid with empty themes and arbitrary history-dependent ties. They compare only terminals generated by the one fixed strategy after each forced action; no count-state or anonymous-continuation assumption is used. A proof of (7) for a strategically selected `p` would imply the corresponding diagonal query budget, but (7) is not established in general.

## Exact finite failure of a stronger diagonal claim

The companion script `diagonal_push_obstruction.py` gives an exact three-provider, five-topic, eight-customer full SPE with all 31 ordered histories and 124 deviations verified. For
\[
p=(10/287,157/287,0,0,120/287),
\]
it computes `p^T K p=1501/1722` and `p^T D=248/287`, so
\[
p^T Kp-p^TD=13/1722>0.
\]
The actual root payoff is `u=7/3`, so this finite certificate does **not** refute Mfull: `p^TKp<u`. It only rules out the stronger shortcut “replace adaptive forced-root payoff by the same-distribution static extra-slot payoff and obtain a nonpositive difference for every `p`.”

The Cartesian obstruction in `cartesian_pair_obstruction.md` separately rules out paying `R` using only coordinatewise local `e` and `J` terms. Both diagnostics leave free nonlocal Mfull/Mpath and Q-CAG-HALF open.
