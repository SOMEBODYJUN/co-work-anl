$$(1/4,1/4),\ (0,1),\ (1/2,3/2),\ (2,3),$$

参数$\eta\in\{0,1/2,1\}$、目标$q\in\{0,1,2\}$。直接由菜单计算

$$C_x=\sum_{i=0}^1z_i\frac{t_i}{p+1+r_i},\quad
H_\eta=\eta Z/\tau+V/\tau,$$

核对$\ q\min(1,d)-C_x+H_\eta=\eta\beta+r_q$。完整计数如下：
