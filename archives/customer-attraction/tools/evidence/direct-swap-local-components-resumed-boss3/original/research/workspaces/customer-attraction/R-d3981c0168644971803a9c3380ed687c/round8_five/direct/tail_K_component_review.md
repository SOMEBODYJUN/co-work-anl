# Independent reconstruction of the last-two K component

2026-10-25. Internal independent analytic reconstruction of the component communicated by tail/root seats. Original five-provider model; not HALF(5), an external review, or a novelty certification.

Fix the actual first three themes and their load q. Write the actual final two as D,E, with profits u4,u5. Define

    f(A)=sum_A 1/(q+1),
    g(A,B)=sum_(A cap B)1/[(q+1)(q+2)],
    Phi(A,B)=f(A)+f(B)-g(A,B),
    M2=max_(A,B)Phi(A,B),
    Gamma=M2-Phi(D,E)>=0.

A common background constant in harmonic potential is omitted. Let T be any theme occurring first in an M2-maximizing ordered pair, and let Q_T be the original sigma's genuine final reply after prefix q and T. Since maximizing the final player's utility is exactly maximizing Phi(T,·), Phi(T,Q_T)=M2 even with arbitrary ties. Put

    delta_T=f(T)-g(T,Q_T),    epsilon_T=u4-delta_T>=0.

The original fourth player's IC gives epsilon_T>=0. A global potential-maximizing pair is a static Nash pair in this fixed background. Hence delta_T is at least the maximum mixed query against the fixed four legal competitors consisting of the three original prefix themes and Q_T, and therefore delta_T>=v5. This is the original oblivious v5; the competitor background is fixed before sampling the query.

Directly,

    Gamma+epsilon_T=f(Q_T)-f(E).

The original actual last player's BR inequality gives

    f(Q_T)-f(E)<=g(D,Q_T)-g(D,E)
      <=sum_(D minus E)1/[(q+1)(q+2)]<=u4/2.

Thus for K=max_T(Gamma+epsilon_T), over the nonempty M2-optimal-first set,

    0<=Gamma<=K<=u4/2.

The finiteness, empty-theme, repeated-theme, zero-customer and tie boundaries cause no problem. The condition

    u1+u2+u3+K>=3v5                              (OPEN)

is sufficient for half: using u4,u5>=v5 gives

    W>=3v5-K+u4+u5>=3v5+u4/2+u5>=9v5/2,

and the existing static theorem OPT5<=9v5 finishes. This reconstructs the component and its sufficient implication only. It neither proves the OPEN condition nor transfers K to other mutually exclusive source histories.

The right side is 3v5, not 3u5. These are different claims. The two-singleton complete SPE in this directory has actual C,C,C,H,H, P_3=1, Gamma=K=0, u5=1/2, and v5=1/3.
