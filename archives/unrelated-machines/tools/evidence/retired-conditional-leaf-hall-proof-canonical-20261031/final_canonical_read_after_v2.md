## 2. New domain, preprocessing, and all degeneracies

The domain is explicit normalized-flat data: nonempty groups and indexed state menus, nonnegative binary rational coefficients with total one per state, and no repeated resource within one indexed state. Zero incidences, duplicate indexed states, and unused resources remain permitted.

The b<epsilon criterion is an exact threshold-unit-demand test, including its strict inequality at the boundary. It correctly puts all syntactic unit-singleton menus into L and also recognizes any other menu with that same threshold property. The new diffuse parking states keep the original helpers outside L at epsilon=1 and, when t>1/epsilon, at the same fixed epsilon. Thus the new gap is not the old singleton-core example with a different declaration of C.

Empty C or empty L is interpreted correctly in the general necessary relaxation. In the projection theorem the nonempty old group set is designated as C, and exactly the added paths are L. This is a real precondition, not automatic elimination in disguise. The automatic version additionally requires every original group to remain non-threshold-unit-demand. The final text states that condition and explains why changing the partition changes the theorem.

The positive-neighbor D_+ operation is clearly distinguished from the original D operation that includes zero-only group incidences. Group ports are shared across all that group's indexed states. Zero jobs are routed exactly as specified and do not become positive usages. The treatment of zero lambda, fractional z, d=0 retained unused resources, d=1 empty matchings, and b_i=1 zero residual transport agrees with the independent reconstruction.
