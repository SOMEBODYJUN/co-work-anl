# ULTIMATE RESEARCH MISSION

# Complete Morrison–Kawamata Cone Conjecture

## Mission

Attempt to prove the complete generalized Morrison–Kawamata cone conjecture for all projective klt Calabi–Yau pairs over the complex numbers, without any restriction on dimension.

The project must address both the absolute and relative formulations.

## Mathematical setting

Let

f:(X,Delta) -> S

be any projective surjective morphism such that (X,Delta) is klt and

K_X + Delta ≡_S 0.

Do not impose a bound on:

- dim X;
- dim S;
- dim(X/S);
- Picard number;
- irregularity;
- deformation type.

## Main Conjecture A: Nef Cone

Prove the existence of a rational polyhedral fundamental domain for the action of

Aut(X/S,Delta)

on the effective relative nef cone.

## Main Conjecture B: Movable Cone

Prove the existence of a rational polyhedral fundamental domain for the action of

PsAut(X/S,Delta)

on the effective relative movable cone.

## Main Conjecture C: Birational Finiteness

Establish the corresponding finiteness of:

1. Minimal models up to isomorphism.
2. Contractions up to isomorphism.

Use the precise definitions and formulations in Li's 2023 paper, Conjecture 1.1.

## Research requirements

1. Audit all existing proofs and known special cases.
2. Separate absolute and relative statements.
3. Identify which dimensions and geometric classes are already solved.
4. Identify the essential missing theorem in the general case.
5. Develop methods that construct the required automorphisms and pseudo-automorphisms.
6. Investigate the relation between generic-fiber cone conjectures and relative cone conjectures.
7. Develop a theory handling degenerate movable cones and nontrivial linear subspaces.
8. Investigate nontrivial relative irregularity.
9. Investigate higher-dimensional Calabi–Yau fibers.
10. Search for counterexamples to overly strong formulations.

## Research architecture

Build a unified proof strategy that can handle:

- K3 surfaces;
- Abelian varieties;
- Hyperkähler varieties;
- Calabi–Yau threefolds;
- Higher-dimensional Calabi–Yau varieties;
- General klt Calabi–Yau pairs;
- Relative Calabi–Yau fibrations.

Existing theorems must be used as benchmarks, not presented as new results.

## PASS CONDITIONS

PASS requires a proof of both the nef and movable cone conjectures in the stated full generality, together with the corresponding birational finiteness conclusions.

No hidden dimension restrictions.

No hidden non-degeneracy assumptions unless explicitly included in a separately stated theorem.

No restriction to K3 fibers or previously known special classes.

A proof of relative dimension three alone is PARTIAL.

A proof of the movable-cone part alone is PARTIAL.

A proof of a special family is PARTIAL.

## Method

Use conventional mathematical proofs, exact calculations, independent mathematical review and complete literature verification.

No Lean or Coq formalization is required.

If the full conjecture cannot be completed, identify the exact strongest theorem that has been proved and the minimal remaining obstruction.

Do not fabricate a proof or claim completion without establishing every required statement.

Start with the full conjecture, not a predetermined low-dimensional restriction.
