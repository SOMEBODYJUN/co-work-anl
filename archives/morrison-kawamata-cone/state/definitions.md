# Fixed scope and conventions

The parent problem is MKC-ULTIMATE, over the complex numbers, with no bound on any dimension, Picard number, irregularity or deformation type. Its exact assignment is mission_original.md. This document distinguishes formulations; it does not weaken that assignment.

For a projective morphism use the real vector space of Cartier divisors modulo numerical equivalence on curves contracted by f, denoted V=N^1(X/S)_R. In particular, do not replace this by N^1(X)_R/f^*N^1(S)_R without proving equality. Let Eff be the cone of finite nonnegative real combinations of effective Cartier divisor classes; it is not its closure. Set

\[
C_A=\operatorname{Nef}(X/S)\cap\operatorname{Eff}(X/S),\qquad
C_B=\overline{\operatorname{Mov}}(X/S)\cap\operatorname{Eff}(X/S).
\]

Use the faithful linear images Γ_A and Γ_B of the corresponding actual automorphism and pseudo-automorphism groups. A kernel acting trivially on V is not an obstruction to the usual numerical formulation.

For the effective-cone target, a rational polyhedral domain Π is a cone generated by finitely many rational vectors, contained in the specified cone, with ΓΠ=C and disjoint interiors for distinct numerical translates. The stronger closed-translate/interior separation and trivial setwise stabilizer are tracked separately when proved. No arbitrary pointedness assumption is imposed; polyhedral cones may contain lines. Interior always means relative interior in the stated linear span.

## Exact comparison with Li (2023)

Reference: Zhan Li, arXiv:2309.04673v1, Conjecture 1.1 and §§2–3, https://arxiv.org/html/2309.04673v1 .

- That conjecture concerns Mov_+ and Amp_+, where C_+=Conv(overline(C)∩V_Q), and allows weak domains. These are recorded as additional, distinct target versions. Equality with C_A or C_B is a proof obligation, not a definition.
- Li's fiber spaces have connected fibers and Q-factorial X; the current user's projective surjective setting does not explicitly include those assumptions. Non-Q-factorial models and the passage through Stein factorization are additional scope obligations, not silently discarded cases.
- Li's notation Bir(X/S,Δ) preserves Supp(Δ). The coefficient-preserving group g_*Δ=Δ is tracked separately. There are finitely many boundary components, so the subgroup fixing every component is finite index in the support-preserving group; this does not by itself prove descent of a strong domain in a degenerate cone.
- Minimal-model and contraction finiteness means unmarked models/targets up to the appropriate isomorphism over S as in Li's definitions. Infinitely many markings or maps to one isomorphic target do not contradict that conclusion.
- The 2023 preprint and the 2026 published JAG paper are separate versions; changes in theorem statements must be checked before transferring a citation.

We retain all requested versions. A complete proof must explicitly bridge any difference, or state the exact strongest theorem and leave the remaining version open. Numerical triviality is not silently changed to a stronger hypothesis; any numerical-to-linear-triviality theorem used must be cited with its hypotheses.

## Absolute versus relative

The absolute problem is S=Spec(C). Generic fiber X_η is over C(S); its geometric generic fiber and a very general complex fiber are different objects. Their automorphism groups, descent data and Picard groups must not be identified without proof.

## Proof status

An existing published theorem is an input with its actual hypotheses. A conditional theorem has an independently correct implication and explicitly open premises. An unreviewed argument remains a candidate. A finite calculation or internal review alone does not prove the full target. No result receives parent PASS unless A, B, both finiteness statements, and the scope/formulation bridges have all been established.
