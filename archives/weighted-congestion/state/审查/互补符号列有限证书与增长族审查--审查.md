# 对象、来源和读取边界

本审查逐项读取两份冻结 JSON，并实读其程序 `tests/audits/weighted_congestion/signed_columns/search_layers.py` 与 `verify.py`，再与 `research/questions/weighted_congestion/signed_columns/TRIAD_AND_EXTENSION.md`、相关 weighted-congestion 正文和既有有限证书比较。库存中的两个路径各有 `legacy-cleanup` 与 `main` 绑定；稳定 ID 与文件 SHA256 为：`8e4b662574b1bfa6c65d`（`screening.json`，SHA256 `0f5e18f9ec24539b9396a7db664e981050318ec92d2723d36af93fd2199ac1b0`）和 `a8dc0b24d1c2319d7e82`（`verification.json`，SHA256 `cb1050c4dd101308552eb5f546890e22a771779a53ee8bbbd7e1cdff9b11dcd9`）。

研究对象是互补资源对：一个 mask 的 0 表示不含该玩家，1/2 表示该玩家使用两种动作；每个 mask 都带有交换 1/2 的互补资源，资源延迟为非负系数多项式。全局比特补集保持每个玩家成本，因此可将 player 0 的动作归一为 0 以减少状态代表，但精确验证仍保留所有状态。对每个状态，记录每个玩家当前成本与单边偏离成本，定义该状态的最佳偏离比为 `max_i C_i(s)/C_i(s^i)`；全状态阈值是这些最大比的最小值。若阈值大于 `alpha`，则每个状态至少有一位玩家的严格 `alpha` 改善，故该固定游戏没有 `alpha`-PNE（端点本身需另行判断，通常只得到对所有 `beta<alpha` 的无 PNE 结论）。

## 1. 三玩家精确 triad

`verification.json` 的 `triad` 使用权重 `(1,1/3,1/9)` 和三条资源：mask `(0,1,1)`、系数 67、degree 2；mask `(1,1,2)`、系数 19、degree 1；mask `(1,2,1)`、系数 10、degree 2。它列出全部 8 个状态、每位玩家成本、偏离成本、比值与 witness，并完成 24 个潜在函数/偏离检查。全状态阈值是

\[
\alpha_3=\frac{3229}{3033}=1.0646224859\ldots.
\]

最小值出现在代表状态 `010` 的 player 0；其余代表行的 witness 也被逐一保存。由于资源支持大小至多为 3，程序另外检查了 `phi(s^i)-phi(s) <= 3 C_i(s^i)-C_i(s)` 的支持势不等式；这是对该资源支持结构的回归检查，不能替代逐状态偏离比证书。这个 triad 是完整的有限精确结论，适合在后续 signed-column 家族中作为 `m=2` 的基元；它没有给出增长下界。

## 2. 四玩家 degree-four 有理证书

`verification.json` 的 `four_player_rounded` 使用整数权重 `(27,9,3,1)`，四条互补资源对的代表 mask 为 `(0,1,1,1)`、`(1,2,1,1)`、`(1,1,2,1)`、`(1,1,1,2)`，对应系数 `251/13^4`、`365/40`、`194/40^4`、`191/40^4`，次数分别 4、1、4、4。全部 16 个状态和 64 个单边偏离都以 `Fraction` 精确计算，支持势检查按最大支持 4 完成。全状态阈值为

\[
L_4=\frac{6077048724448}{5133066349015}=1.18390223528\ldots>\frac{59}{50}.
\]

因此这个具体 degree-4 游戏没有 `beta`-PNE（对所有 `beta<L_4`）。阈值在状态 `0111` 及其补集达到，故不能把严格不等式直接延伸到 `beta=L_4`。这是一个固定四玩家、固定四资源对、非负多项式的完整有限证书；它可作为 degree 至少 4 的常数下界实例，但不能被解释为随 degree 增长的 `Omega` 改善，也不能替代目标规模的全状态证明。

与旧问题文档相比，独有内容是把四玩家“rounded”系数全部 rationalize 后由程序重建，保留了每一行的精确比值，而不是只留一个小数近似。它和 triad 共用同一个互补符号列接口，但不是同一模型参数。

## 3. 浮点 screening 的真实证据等级

`screening.json` 是 `search_layers.py` 用 SciPy/HiGHS 的混合整数优化发现数据。对固定 `n,q,d,alpha`，程序取 degree 集合 `{0,1,2,4,8,...,d}`（按脚本规则），在非负系数单纯形上最大化一个浮点 margin；每个补集状态/玩家组用二元 witness 选择至少一个可改善玩家。优化状态、时间限制、归一化系数和 `actual_minmax` 都写入 JSON，但它们不证明精确不可行或全局最优。

七个记录的边界如下：

| n | q | d | alpha | solver 状态 | 浮点 margin | actual minmax |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 3 | 1/3 | 2 | 1.06 | optimal | 0.0062474 | 1.0703288 |
| 4 | 1/3 | 4 | 1.10 | optimal | 0.0419450 | 1.1854848 |
| 4 | 3/4 | 8 | 1.10 | optimal | 0.0012501 | 1.1064565 |
| 4 | 1/2 | 16 | 1.20 | optimal | 0.0105759 | 1.2516051 |
| 5 | 1/2 | 16 | 1.20 | time limit | 0.0046969 | 1.2352951 |
| 4 | 4/5 | 32 | 1.30 | optimal, zero margin | approximately 0 | not recorded |
| 5 | 4/5 | 32 | 1.30 | time limit | 1.4×10^-6 | 0.6999605 |

The n=5, q=4/5, d=32 candidate is a concrete false witness: after rationalization and exact recomputation its threshold is below 1 and `passes_exact=false`; the displayed `actual_minmax<1` already exposes the failure. The n=4, q=4/5, d=32 run has zero margin, which is only a failed discovery attempt, not an impossibility result. The n=5, q=1/2, d=16 run reached a time limit, yet the candidate returned by the solver passes exact rational verification; its solver status remains time-limited and should not be rewritten as an optimal certificate.

## 4. Rationalized screening and exact verification

`verify.py` reconstructs each candidate using `Fraction`, converts the floating q and coefficients through bounded denominators, regenerates both members of each complementary resource pair, and evaluates every state and every unilateral deviation. Of the six screening runs that returned coefficients, five pass exact threshold comparison and one fails:

- `(n,q,d,target)=(3,1/3,2,1.06)`: exact threshold `4209097360555803/3932527457512147`, pass;
- `(4,1/3,4,1.1)`: exact threshold `2092655597163282822976774031697961744736/1765231914498872014388010633607918713065`, pass;
- `(4,3/4,8,1.1)`: exact threshold `883419280012483133556137075893274099471605765663318400/798422060210062801998296510184827106358941405992754933`, pass;
- `(4,1/2,16,1.2)`: exact threshold `7760334739251944788891561014825395471733217484589988/6200306021844924174765630508126815018314635269836125`, pass;
- `(5,1/2,16,1.2)`: exact threshold `12981379024515438690916650190044910895048697020635790133909078375287/10508727455978930312021817657946182502276458853837405550649294695790`, pass;

The exact verifier therefore supplies finite certificates only for the five passing candidates, and the triad/four-player entries are the stable named objects. The rationalization step is part of the mathematical boundary: a floating coefficient vector is not the exact input until its rationalized certificate passes every state and deviation check.

## 5. Growing-support family and the open proof obligation

The related question document defines the full-support signed-column family with one heavy player of weight `h` and light weights `w_1,...,w_m`. There is one complementary pair with mask `(0,1,...,1)` and one pair `B_j` for each light player, with arbitrary nonnegative polynomial latencies of degree at most `d`. For a normalized state `S` of light players on action 0, it defines `W_S`, `W`, `delta_j(S)=w_j(1-2 1_{j in S})`, and the exact loads `X_j(S)=h+W_S+delta_j(S)`, `Y_j(S)=W-W_S-delta_j(S)`. The heavy cost is `sum_j f_j(X_j(S))`; light costs use the common `A` term and one own-side `f_i` term, with the heavy deviation and `S symmetric-difference {i}` formulas explicitly given.

Those formulas are a reusable exact interface and show that support can grow with `m`. The required theorem is stronger: choose `m(d)`, all weights and all latency coefficients, then prove for every subset `S` that either the heavy deviation or a light deviation has ratio at least a target `L(d)`. The source explicitly leaves this obligation open. A selected improvement cycle, positive finite screening margin, or existence of a full-support family does not prove the universal `for every S` statement. The resourcewise-local copies obstruction remains relevant: independent triad copies do not automatically produce a growing lower bound, while the full-support family only escapes that particular obstruction and still needs a uniform all-state proof.

## 与先有成果的关系、用途和缺口

1. **与三玩家 triad 和四玩家有限对象：** 这两个精确证书应作为独立固定对象接入 weighted-congestion 的有限下界档案。它们证明指定实例的阈值，不证明家族增长。
2. **与循环时钟/多类组装：** signed-column 资源对是另一种互补支持结构；不能把其有限阈值和 cyclic-clone 的 D80/D91 预算、或多类资源修复，直接合并成同一个编译器结论。
3. **与文献比较：** 相关问题文档实读并引用 Christodoulou et al. arXiv:2002.07466v3 §3/Theorem 1/equation (3) 作为已有构造的比较背景；本批没有完成全文献新颖性检索，因此只保留“相对该具体构造的有限比较”，不写新颖性结论。
4. **复现用途：** `verify.py` 无 SciPy 依赖，可从仓库根目录直接重放 triad、四玩家 certificate 和 rationalized candidates；`search_layers.py` 需要 NumPy/SciPy，且时间限制和浮点容差只适合作为发现阶段。
5. **开放缺口：** full-support family 的统一 all-state 下界、目标规模的参数选择、不同 q/权重的精确可行域、端点是否可达、以及与已有增长下界的严格比较都仍开放。失败 candidate 必须保留为反例/障碍，不能删除或改写为成功。

**处置结论：** `screening.json` 保留为浮点候选与失败机制证据；`verification.json` 保留为五个通过候选、一个失败候选，以及三玩家/四玩家完整有理证书。本文件整合独有的阈值、状态量词、rationalization 规则和 full-support 开放义务，供第三阶段建立来源关系边使用。
