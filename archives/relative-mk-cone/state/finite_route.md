# Finite-stabilizer route and its precise unpaid premise

## Uniform geometric premise

For every fibre space in the unchanged problem contract, its actual effective movable cone \(C_e\) admits a full-dimensional rational polyhedral weak fundamental domain \(P\) for the numerical action image, with finite \(\operatorname{Stab}_\Gamma(P)\).

**Status: OPEN.** This sufficient premise has not been proved. Existing weak results on \(M_+\) do not establish it on \(C_e\). The finite clause is sufficient for this route, not claimed necessary for every other approach.

## Abstract refinement

A weak rational polyhedral domain with finite stabilizer for a faithful rational linear action can be refined to one strong domain. The exact statement and complete proof are in `proof.tex`, Lemma `lem:finite`. A separate reader checked the worker's full derivation; see `convex_reduction_review.md`. The canonical object is conservatively a candidate pending the formal frozen-publication procedure; no originality is claimed.

## Joint implication

The uniform geometric premise AND the abstract refinement imply the original strong-domain target, on its exact cone and numerical group. For each target object apply the premise, then refine its domain by the lemma; the lemma provides coverage, rational polyhedrality, stronger boundary separation and trivial stabilizer. No quotient/cone-equality assertion is used without proof. The geometric premise remains wholly unpaid. This is a conditional route, not a proof of the main theorem.
