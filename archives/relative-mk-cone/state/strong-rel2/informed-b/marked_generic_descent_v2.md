# Rational descent of the absolute generic numerical cone

This is a proof of an independent source bridge for the proposal, constructed by the second reviewer during adversarial review. The proof gives a covering cone for the actual field automorphisms. The argument only concerns the absolute generic surface. If adopted in a new candidate, it requires review by someone other than the writer of this note.

Let k=C(S), let F be a smooth projective geometrically integral surface over k, and let B be a rational effective boundary defined over k such that the geometric pair is klt and K_F+B is Q-linearly trivial. Let P denote the Albanese torsor of F under D=Alb⁰(F). Write W=NS(F_bar k)_Q ⊗ R and let O⊂W be its open ample cone. Use the lattice NS modulo torsion throughout. The model and the argument also work for projective geometrically klt Q-factorial surfaces where the indicated Albanese is defined. Only the smooth case is needed for the proposal.

**Geometric covering.** Identify the abstract algebraic closure of k with C. Totaro, Theorem 4.1 in “The cone conjecture for Calabi-Yau pairs in dimension two,” gives a rational polyhedral cone Q contained in the geometric nef-effective cone, covering it under the geometric coefficient-preserving automorphism group M. Totaro §2 explicitly defines this group by preservation of the divisor. In particular Q⊂O⁺, the rational hull of the closed ample cone, since its rational generators are nef. Denote the faithful numerical image of M on W by Γ. Then (O⁺,Γ) is of polyhedral type and Γ is finitely generated by Looijenga, Proposition–Definition 4.1 and Corollary 4.15.

**A finite Galois-invariant marking.** Over the algebraic closure, the connected component A of M maps isogenously onto the translations D of the Albanese torsor P by Jinsong Xu, Theorem 4.5(1)–(3). Fix a finite separable closed point of F of degree n, and write z₁,…,zₙ for its images on P of the n geometric conjugates, with repetitions allowed. Let Z consist of the points z on P for which Σᵢ(zᵢ−z)=0 in D. This equation is independent of an origin and stable under Galois. In geometric coordinates it is a fibre of multiplication by n on D. Thus Z is one nonempty finite D[n]-coset. For q=0 the Albanese torsor is a point and this construction still applies. Every g∈M acts on P by an affine isomorphism whose linear part preserves D[n], so gZ is another D[n]-coset. The surjectivity of A→D on geometric points gives an element of A translating gZ back to Z. Connected automorphisms act trivially on the Néron–Severi group. It follows that the subgroup M_Z⊂M preserving Z has full image Γ.

The kernel of M_Z→Γ is finite. The group scheme preserving B and the numerical class of one fixed integral ample line bundle has finite type: the graphs embed in one Hilbert scheme with fixed polynomial, by numerical invariance of the Hilbert polynomial, and the component-preservation equations cut out a finite union of closed conditions. Its identity component is A. In each of its finitely many geometric components, any two members preserving Z differ by an element of A preserving Z. Such an element maps to the finite translation group D[n] under the isogeny A→D, so there are only finitely many. In particular M_Z is finitely generated, by lifting generators of Γ and adjoining the finite kernel.

**Descent on the interior.** The subgroup M_Z is preserved by the absolute Galois group. Choose one finite Galois extension splitting the finitely many generators of M_Z as morphisms and the geometric Néron–Severi action, and write H for its Galois group. Both actions factor through H. The fixed automorphisms M_Z^H are genuine k-automorphisms of (F,B): invariance of their graphs is effective Galois descent. The numerical representation of the semidirect product M_Z⋊H has finite kernel, since there are finitely many choices of h∈H and for fixed h the fibre in M_Z has cardinality at most the finite numerical kernel. Its image preserves O and the integral lattice.

For this group the set
\[
    \mathcal S=\{a\in M_Z\rtimes H\mid Q\cap aQ\cap O\ne\varnothing\}
\]
is finite. This is the Siegel property, Looijenga, Theorem 3.8, applied to the finitely many pairs of relative interiors of faces of Q having interior points of O. In that case the smallest face of O⁺ containing such a relative interior is O⁺ itself, whose pointwise stabilizer in the faithful linear image is trivial. Pullback through a finite-kernel representation preserves finiteness. Looijenga also writes this exact finite-set deduction in the proof of Proposition 4.6.

For every x∈O∩W^H choose g∈M_Z with y=g⁻¹x∈Q. For each h∈H the conjugate g⁻¹hg fixes y∈Q∩O, hence belongs to the finite set above. Only finitely many labelled tuples (g⁻¹hg) indexed by h∈H can occur. Choose one g_j for each tuple that occurs. If g and g_j give the same tuple then gg_j⁻¹ commutes with all of H in the actual semidirect product, so gg_j⁻¹∈M_Z^H. It follows that the rational polyhedral cones g_j Q∩W^H, in a finite list, cover O∩W^H up to the actual k-automorphisms M_Z^H. Here the intersection is with the fixed space in the full geometric W, not with a quotient of the automorphism group.

Restriction identifies NS(F/k)_Q with (NS(F_bar k)_Q)^H: injectivity is numerical descent against finite-extension curves; conversely a rational invariant divisor class can be represented geometrically over a finite Galois extension and its trace represents a positive integral multiple on F. If the geometric class has an effective rational representative, the trace is effective. In particular every rational ray generator of g_jQ∩W^H has a k-effective rational representative. To see rational effectivity already over the geometric field, express this rational effective numerical vector as a finite nonnegative real combination of prime divisor classes and solve the rational linear system for a nonnegative rational solution. Nefness and ampleness are stable under field extension. Consequently the convex hull cone Q_k generated by this finite list lies in D_k=Nef(F/k)∩Eff(F/k), is rational polyhedral and has translates covering the full k-ample cone.

Looijenga, Proposition–Definition 4.1, now gives the whole rational hull of the closed k-ample cone as the union of the translates of Q_k. Its containment in D_k follows from k-effectivity of Q_k and invariance. The reverse containment follows from the fixed-support decomposition of a nef effective real divisor on a smooth surface into rational nef effective divisors, or by taking the geometric Totaro cone, expressing each actual nef effective class as a nonnegative sum of rational geometric nef classes and averaging over the finite group H. This proves equality and gives the required rational covering for the group of k-automorphisms preserving B. The finite-index extension-to-the-total-space argument and the pointed chamber refinement in the full candidate now apply verbatim.
