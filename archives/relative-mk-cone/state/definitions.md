# Definitions and convention ledger

## Fixed notation

\(V=N^1(X/S)_{\mathbb R}\), with its rational Cartier-class structure, and \(\Gamma\) is the action image, not the possibly nonfaithful abstract pseudoautomorphism group. A rational polyhedral cone is a closed convex cone generated by finitely many rational vectors; it may contain lines.

Keep three cones separate:
\[
 M=\overline{\operatorname{Mov}}(X/S),\quad C_e=M\cap\operatorname{Eff}(X/S),\quad
 M_+=\operatorname{Conv}(M\cap N^1(X/S)_{\mathbb Q}).
\]
The target uses \(C_e\). The bar on \(\overline{\operatorname{Mov}}^e\) does not assert that its intersection with the effective cone is closed.

A weak domain covers the specified cone and satisfies \(gP\cap\operatorname{Int}(P)=\varnothing\) unless \(gP=P\). A strong domain additionally has trivial setwise stabilizer. Full-dimensionality must be established, so empty ambient interiors cannot give a vacuous success. If \(V=0\), treat the trivial image group separately.

## Lineality and quotient obligations

\(L_e=C_e\cap(-C_e)\) and \(W=M\cap(-M)\) need not be equal. In the abstract cone \(C_0=\{(x,y):y>0\}\cup\{0\}\), \(L_e=0\) while its closure has lineality \(\mathbb R(1,0)\). Thus quotienting by \(L_e\) need not produce the nondegenerate open cone required in the literature.

Do not assume \(W\subset C_e\), \(C_e=q^{-1}q(C_e)\), \(C_e=M_+\), or faithfulness after quotienting. Each assertion is a separate mathematical obligation. The quotient-action kernel is \(\ker(\Gamma\to\operatorname{GL}(V/W))\), which can remain after taking the action image on \(V\).
