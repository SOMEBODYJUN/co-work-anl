# Counterexample audit

No geometric counterexample satisfying all assumptions of the original target has been verified.

| Candidate/check | Established fact | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Closed half-plane with integral shear | Quotient domain exists, but every convex covering cone upstairs is the whole half-plane; no strong domain exists. Complete proof in proof.tex. | Purely linear example, not a realized terminal Calabi–Yau fibration. It blocks a universal lifting lemma only. |
| Open half-plane plus zero with the same shear | The closed sector `0<=x<=y` is a strong domain. | Shows why adding/removing the effective boundary changes the answer; not a geometric proof. |
| Kawamata Example 3.8(2) | Primary example must be read with its local/global hypotheses. It distinguishes effective movable cone from rational hull. | That distinction alone does not refute the strong effective-cone conjecture. |
| Finite quotient-action kernel | A finite stabilizer can be refined by rational half-spaces; proof.tex proves this. | Finiteness and cone comparison remain geometric obligations. |
| Non-Q-factorial base | Explicitly allowed by the target. | No reduction to a Q-factorial base is accepted without descent of spaces, cones and group actions. |
| V=0 | The action image is trivial and `{0}` gives the domain. | Does not settle relative dimension zero with positive relative Picard rank. |

Pro 2 must verify projectivity, terminal/Q-factorial total space, the correct boundary and relative canonical triviality for any proposed geometric counterexample, then compute the exact effective cone and action image. A model on the closure or rational hull alone cannot refute the requested statement.
