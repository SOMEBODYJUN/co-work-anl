**Zero and low-dimensional cases.** For \(\dim X\le1\), Section 1 treats the point, a curve mapping isomorphically to a normal curve, and the degree ray for a curve over a point, including all permitted boundary coefficients. For \(\dim X\ge2,\ r=0\), connected fibres give a birational contraction, hence \(\Gamma=1\) and every divisor is vertical. Here \(L=0,\ \mathrm{Eff}=V\) and the vertical effective-polyhedral-intersection assertion with the rational cone \(V\) gives \(C\) rational polyhedral. For \(\dim X\ge2,\ r=1,2,\ U=0\), \(\pi\) identifies \(p(C)\) and its faithful action with the generic cone and group; lift \(B\) directly. The table applies in the remaining cases. In every klt case §1 selects a same-support rational working log-Calabi–Yau boundary. The action on the original C is unchanged. Clearing its adjoint denominator gives a nonzero coefficientwise-rounded section for each actual effective real perturbation, as required by the ordinary relative Kodaira dimension in Z.
