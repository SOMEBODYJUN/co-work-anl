# Third manuscript: venue and submission-format review (2026-10-25)

## Decision

**Primary venue: ACM Transactions on Economics and Computation (TEAC); fallback: SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics (SIDMA).** The principal result is an all-state weighted-congestion nonexistence construction at factor `d - O(log² d)`, matching the leading asymptotic constant of the imported `d` existence guarantee. Its game-theoretic object and structural realization mechanism fit TEAC's intended readership. The positive potential and social-selection results have conditional, separately stated scopes. This is an editorial recommendation, not a prediction of acceptance or a priority certificate. The finite-degree exact threshold, optimal additive gap, and general polynomial-time equilibrium search remain open.

The review source now uses the ACM `acmart` `manuscript,review,anonymous` single-column mode and `\acmJournal{TEAC}`. The `anonymous` option is an interim review presentation because actual author metadata has not been supplied; it is **not** asserted to be a TEAC-specific requirement. The official ACM author instructions specify `manuscript` for review; the journal production format is `acmsmall`, to be applied only at production. The current ACM template listing is v2.20. The locally compiled PDF used the tagged v2.20 upstream *development repository* snapshot solely for layout testing, because the production CTAN/ACM class could not be fetched in this runtime. Before submission, compile from the current official production package and check the TEAC-specific instructions in the submission portal. Do not submit the local testing class as a production dependency.

Sources checked: [ACM author instructions](https://www.acm.org/publications/authors/submissions), [ACM LaTeX guide](https://authors.acm.org/proceedings/production-information/preparing-your-article-with-latex), [CTAN package](https://ctan.org/pkg/acmart), [TEAC journal](https://dl.acm.org/journal/teac). ## Mathematical and literature boundary

Caragiannis--Fanelli, [author version, Theorem 2 and Corollary 5/Theorem 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1902.07173), formally use positive monomial latencies, prove a `d`-approximate potential, obtain an improved high-congestion potential, and give an approximate price-of-stability bound. The `d` guarantee for finite nonnegative polynomial sums follows by splitting each resource into per-monomial parallel copies and using an additive potential for constant copies; the manuscript states this as a deduction rather than attributing the enlarged domain verbatim to their formal model. Christodoulou--Gairing--Giannakopoulos--Poças--Waldmann, [author version §7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.07466), explicitly leave the gap between their order `sqrt(d)/log d` lower bound and the `d` upper bound. The current theorem closes its **leading asymptotic factor**. No claim about exact finite `d` or the prior paper's complexity results is made.

The manuscript's small-share factor uses its own all-state occupied-load condition. Its social-quality statements concern a realized front and require the separate social-decrease hypothesis to compare with the full-game optimum; potential use and generic stability--cost tradeoffs are not claimed as first inventions. A broader novelty/priority search and external refereeing have not occurred.

## Other venue and readiness checks

SIDMA remains a reasonable discrete mathematics fallback. Its [author instructions](https://epubs.siam.org/journal/sidma/instructions-for-authors) encourage SIAM macros; [current SIAM class](https://epubs.siam.org/pb-assets/macros/standard/siamart251216.cls) would require a new format build and front-matter checks if the fallback is used.

MOR is a plausible game-theory venue, but this version centers the equilibrium existence threshold rather than a separate OR application. The [live MOR instructions](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/moor/submission-guidelines) say normally no more than 40 pages at 1.5 spacing, excluding references, with up to 45 pages at editorial discretion. They impose no 35-page minimum. Review uses one combined PDF for main text and companion; authorship is removed for soft double-anonymous review. The earlier round-1 35--45-page lower-bound and separate-companion statements were stale and are explicitly corrected here.

Remaining submission particulars: genuine author/affiliation information, the venue portal's specific anonymity and metadata rules, an official production-class recompile, and the authors' final verification of all mathematical and literature claims. The current PDF and source are a review-format manuscript, not a submitted article.
