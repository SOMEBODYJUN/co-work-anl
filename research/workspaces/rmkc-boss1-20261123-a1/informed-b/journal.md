## Generic Picard quotient and its covariance

Write F=X_η, k=C(S), and T for the rational span of vertical divisors in V_Q. Restriction of Cartier divisors, common rational functions, and Q-factoriality identify V_Q/T with Pic(F)_Q modulo the restrictions of f-numerically trivial Q-divisors on X. Every divisor on F extends by horizontal closure and a Cartier multiple; the kernel of ordinary Pic restriction consists of classes with vertical representative. Relative numerically trivial divisors restrict to Pic⁰(F)_Q (numerically trivial modulo torsion). Points of the Picard scheme versus line bundles differ by a torsion Brauer obstruction, immaterial over Q. Thus the kernel U of V_Q/T→N¹(F)_Q is a quotient of Pic⁰(F)(k)_Q by a specified subspace K₀, and this identification needs neither base Q-factoriality nor equality of absolute and relative Néron–Severi quotients.

**Endomorphism stability when F is an abelian torsor.** Let F be a torsor for A/k. The period of its class τ divides some m, since it is split by a finite separable extension and one may take corestriction. For a∈End_k(A), choose n≥1 so u=1+mn a is an isogeny; the degree (or determinant on a Tate module) polynomial is nonzero at 0. Then u_*τ=τ, so there is a u-equivariant k-morphism F→F. The choice differs by a translation and is immaterial on Pic⁰. Spread it to an S-rational map X⇢X, resolve its graph as p:Z→X birational, q:Z→X a morphism. If D≡_S0 then q*D≡_S0. The difference p*p_*q*D−q*D is p-exceptional and p-numerically trivial, hence vanishes by the negativity lemma in both directions. Therefore p_*q*D≡_S0. A vertical divisor also stays vertical under this operation. Thus u^∨ preserves K₀, and subtracting 1 and dividing by mn proves stability under a^∨. The proof is by correspondences and does not require that a itself extend as a pseudo-automorphism. With a rational ample polarization H of F, the operators τ_d=λ_H^{-1}λ_d are Rosati self-adjoint and preserve the corresponding quotient of A(k)_Q.

**Uniform torsor origin and affine offsets.** Choose a finite Galois splitting extension and a geometric point p of F in it. The normalized trace of p is a Galois-fixed rational point p₀ of the affine Q-space associated to the A(k̄)-torsor F(k̄). On F identified with A using p, the map s_p(d)=(L+i_p^*L)/2 is a rational lift of d∈NS(F)_Q independent of the chosen L of that numerical class, where i_p is reflection about p. Translating p by b changes s_p(d) by −λ_d(b) for the convention λ_d(b)=t_b^*L−L (because i_{p+b}=t_{2b}i_p). Average these sections over the Galois orbit of p. This defines a rational section s_{p₀} on the defined-over-k NS space; finite norm descends the classes. For an affine automorphism g, g^*s_{p₀}(d)=s_{g^{-1}p₀}(g^*d). Its difference from s_{p₀}(g^*d) is λ_{g^*d} times the single rational displacement p₀−g^{-1}p₀. If the trace has degree m, m(g^{-1}p₀−p₀) is in A(k). The denominator is therefore uniform for the entire group and the displacement is independent of d. The linear parts preserving an ample numerical H form a finite group of group automorphisms of the polarized abelian variety, and each fixes τ_d if it acts trivially on generic NS. These facts do not assert that a space group splits as an integral semidirect product.

**Actual effective boundary in the abelian case.** Suppose a rational line bundle L on F is effective, with numerical class d. After a finite extension one may treat actual integral L, and work on A with any chosen origin. Its numerical class is nef. Put N=ker(λ_d)^0. On a general coset of N, a nonzero section restricts nontrivially and has degree zero numerically, hence trivializes the restricted bundle. Under the standard translations identifying different cosets with N, the restrictions agree in Pic⁰(N), since im λ_d is the annihilator of N. The reflection and translated reflections used in s_{p₀} keep this trivial restriction. Consequently L−s_{p₀}(d) is in im(λ_d:A→A^∨) on geometric points, and is in λ_d(A(k)_Q) rationally by lifting over a finite extension and tracing. The statement persists in U. A real effective class is a finite nonnegative sum of effective rational classes L_i. On a finite-dimensional real module with a metric making the induced τ_{d_i} positive semidefinite, im(Σ a_i τ_{d_i})=Σ_{a_i>0}im τ_{d_i}, so the real class satisfies the analogous image condition. A rational metric and its compatibility with the quotient must be proved; End-stability alone is not positivity.

## Unpaid steps as of this checkpoint

1. Construct a compatible positive rational inner product on the finite-dimensional quotient module and verify each induced nef τ_d is positive semidefinite; verify q=1 analogue of the uniform affine action and boundary assertion. The abelian calculations above do not silently extend to bielliptic or ruled surfaces.
2. Check finite-index lift of generic translations for the full relative pair with arbitrary vertical boundary, even when restricted generic Δ is zero; the blanket wording of Li II Lemma 2.6 must not be applied to an arbitrary birational map ignoring the boundary. Justify all denominators and actual translation subgroup, not just its rational rank. Identify the exact projected cone whose kernel is being separated.
3. Combine marked-geography control of vertical directions with the generic Dirichlet inequalities and actual effective-cone intersection. The geometric cone finiteness, weak rational-hull theorem and an unverified polyhedron do not themselves solve this. No global proof/counterexample is claimed here.

## Closed obligations in the second pass

**1. Relative nongeneric data versus the precise projected cone.** For a relatively movable Cartier divisor, restriction to the generic fibre has no fixed divisorial components. On a projective surface any such class is nef, since a representative avoiding a given irreducible curve has nonnegative degree on it. The same holds after finite scalar extension; relative basepoint-free locus and linear systems commute at the generic point. Continuity gives the implication for the closed relative movable cone. Relative effectivity gives a generic effective representative. Conversely, if a generic integral L has a movable multiple, use a positive multiple and choose H∈|L|; its horizontal closure D has no vertical components. For a horizontal prime that might be fixed, choose H'∈|L| avoiding its generic prime and let D' be the horizontal closure. Then D−D'−div(α) is vertical. Adding the pullback of a sufficiently large effective Cartier divisor of the projective base makes this vertical correction effective. Thus a member of the relative system avoids that horizontal prime. There can be no fixed vertical prime since D itself has no vertical component. This proves D relatively movable, precisely the argument of Li II, proof of Thm. 1.3, Step 1, pp. 22–23. The argument produces relative divisors by closure without applying any birational contraction.

A generic effective R-divisor L whose class is nef decomposes in Pic(F)_R into a finite nonnegative sum of rational effective nef divisors. Indeed, write its finite support after geometric scalar extension. On a surface, every other curve intersects every support component nonnegatively, so among divisors with this support nefness is given by the finitely many rational inequalities against those components and nonnegativity of coefficients; Galois-invariant coefficients are a rational linear subspace. Rational polyhedral decomposition gives the assertion. On a curve it is immediate. For every rational summand, surface abundance applied to the klt perturbation Δ'_F+εL, where Δ' is a rational boundary with the same support and log canonical class Q-linearly trivial, gives semiampleness. Basepoint-freeness of a multiple descends by flatness. Thus horizontal lifting applies to all summands.

Write D_gen for the effective nef cone in N¹(F). Moraga–Stark 3.8 supplies a rational polyhedral generic domain, so pointed Looijenga (Li–Zhao Prop. 3.3) shows D_gen = Nef(F)⁺. For any rational class in the closed relative movable cone its restriction is rational nef, hence in D_gen. Conversely, every class of D_gen is generated by rational effective nef classes which lift by the preceding paragraph. Thus restriction of Mov(X/S)⁺ is D_gen. Under the original terminal-pair assumption, Li II Thm. 1.8 applies with the full generic group by Moraga–Stark and low-dimensional good minimal models; its conclusion concerns the actual relative group. Restricting its weak chamber gives a rational polyhedral covering cone for D_gen by the actual relative image. Since D_gen has nondegenerate closure, Looijenga gives a strong base chamber there. This use of Mov⁺ never identifies Mov⁺ with the original C.

For the full Picard quotient V/T the same horizontal lifting identifies the image of C with the classes of actual generic nef-effective real line divisors in Pic(F)_R modulo K₀. For each rational effective nef d the translation orbit of any one effective L above d has convex hull the entire affine space of L + im T_d: convex hull of a lattice of full rank in this image is the entire image, with a rational representation for rational points. The subspace in question is the Q-span of λ_d of the rational translation group, not an arbitrary ambient lattice. For real d, write d as a nonnegative sum of rational effective nef classes and use im(sum of PSD operators)=sum of images of terms with positive coefficient. In the abelian-torsor case the converse constraint on actual effective classes was proved above. The q=1 converse is proved below. Thus no generic Picard direction is added to the actual effective boundary.

**2. Abelian case — rational positive metric on the visible quotient.** With H an ample polarization, let E=End⁰_k(A), a finite-dimensional semisimple rational algebra with positive Rosati involution †. Identify the trace convention with left multiplication on E. On the regular left module put (v,w)=Tr_Q(L_{v†w}); this is a positive definite rational form, by positivity of the Rosati involution (the algebra trace is a positive rational weighting of the reduced traces of its simple factors). The adjoint of left a is left a† by associativity. Embed the finite-dimensional rational left module A(k)_Q modulo λ_H^{-1}K₀ into E^n by semisimplicity and restrict the sum of those forms. Each τ_d is self-adjoint. For a rational nef d the endomorphism τ_d has only nonnegative real eigenvalues: descend all finite data to a finitely generated characteristic-zero field embedded into C and realize λ_H^{-1}λ_d as a semipositive Hermitian operator in the faithful complex analytic representation. On every rational E-module the minimal polynomial divides the faithful minimal polynomial, so τ_d is positive semidefinite there. Real effective nef combinations preserve semipositivity. Any required finite linear holonomy fixing H and the relevant d commutes with the induced τ_d and an average keeps these forms rational and compatible.

**3. Elliptic connected orbit group, including relative curves and nonabelian surface fibres.** Put B=Aut⁰(F,SuppΔ_F), an elliptic curve if h¹(F,O)=1; Li II Thm. 4.1 gives that the orbit-to-Albanese homomorphism B→Alb(F) is an isogeny after algebraic closure, hence over k. Each geometric orbit map o_x:B→F induces the same isogeny j:Pic⁰(F)→B^∨, independently of x, by functoriality of Alb and translation-invariance of Pic⁰. Choose a closed point over a finite Galois extension, average all o_{σx}^*, and descend by trace to a Q-linear map r:Pic(F)_Q→Pic(B)_Q restricting to j. The integer degree of o_x^*L depends only on the numerical class d and not on x (the orbit maps form an algebraic family on connected F). Denote it a(d). With P₁ the class of O_B(0), define w₀(L)=j^{-1}(r(L)−a(d)P₁). This is a rational projection onto Pic⁰(F)(k)_Q. The formula for translating L by b∈B(k) follows by restriction to any orbit: j(t_b^*L−L)=a(d) λ_{P₁}(b). This is the needed single scalar function for all classes. It is nonnegative on the generic nef-effective cone and positive on an ample class.

If L is effective nef with a(d)=0, decompose it into semiample rational summands with the same zero orbit degree. Every positive globally generated multiple pulls back to a globally generated degree-zero line on every B-orbit, hence a trivial line bundle. Thus w₀(L)=0. In particular any such d admits just one nef-effective Picard lift over Q, and the initial complementary section s₀ of w₀ takes that value. For H the subgroup acting trivially on generic NS (using its preimage on F), H modulo its subgroup of B(k)-translations is finite because the stabilizer of the numerical class of an ample bundle in the automorphisms of a projective pair is finite type and has B as its neutral component. On d in ker a the translations act trivially on the entire Picard fibre. Average s₀ restricted to ker a over this finite quotient, to get a rational H-equivariant splitting there. On null effective d, each translated initial section is already itself, by the uniqueness just proved. Extend the resulting splitting to generic NS_Q. The displacement in Pic⁰/K₀ of h*s(d)−s(d) now vanishes on ker a, and so equals a(d)b_h for a single b_h∈U_Q independent of d. The linear holonomy on U is finite; an averaged rational positive metric suffices since T_d is scalar. Translation offsets form a full lattice by item 4; remaining offsets lie in finitely many cosets. This construction does not require an integral splitting.

**4. Extending generic translations and obtaining an actual full lattice.** The boundary is effective, with Q-factorial X. Choose effective rational Δ' with the same support, klt (terminal when the original pair is terminal) and K_X+Δ'~_Q f^*B_S. To see rational feasibility, expand the given relative R-linear relation into finitely many principal and pulled-back Cartier divisors and solve the resulting rational linear equations near the original real coefficient vector. Positivity of the original nonzero coefficients and klt/terminal openness under small perturbations on a fixed support retain the required singularities (Kollár–Mori, Cor. 2.35). Clear denominators so r(K_X+Δ') is linearly equivalent to the pullback of an integral Cartier divisor D_S. Choose a rational r-pluricanonical form Φ on X with div_X(Φ)+rΔ'=f^*D_S. Its relative generic restriction, after removing a fixed top form from the function field k, spans H⁰(F,ω_F^[r](rΔ'_F)). The connected abelian group B fixes each boundary component and its coefficient and acts algebraically on this one-dimensional space; the resulting character to G_m is trivial. Therefore every b∈B(k) fixes the actual rational Φ. For every divisorial valuation v the log discrepancy equals 1+(ord_v Φ−ord_v f^*D_S)/r and is consequently invariant under b. If (X,Δ') is terminal, the valuations with a≤1 are exactly the prime divisors on X. Hence b is an isomorphism in codimension one; its invariance of discrepancies preserves coefficients of Δ' and thus SuppΔ. This repairs the hazard of using Li II Lemma 2.6 literally for arbitrary birational maps forgetting the boundary.

Even with the weaker auxiliary hypothesis that the pair is merely klt and X terminal, a finite-index lifting statement follows. The exceptional valuations with a≤1 form a finite set by Kollár–Mori Prop. 2.36. If such a valuation is horizontal, regularity of the generic automorphism ensures all its transforms remain exceptional by codimension on F; if it is vertical, any transform that is a prime on X lies in the inverse image of the same proper closed subset Z of S. That inverse image has only finitely many divisorial irreducible components. Each exceptional low-discrepancy valuation has finite orbit, so a finite-index subgroup preserves the finite exceptional set. On the complement among all valuations with a≤1 one recovers all primes on X; their discrepancies preserve boundary support. The same reasoning works for a finite-index subgroup of the *full* generic automorphism group preserving the support: first fix the finitely many boundary components and their coefficients, then apply Fujino–Gongyo, “Log pluricanonical representations and the abundance conjecture”, Thm. 1.1, to the generic pair over the algebraic closure. The induced scalar on the relative generic form is a root of unity, hence a complex constant, and does not alter the orders in the formula. An abstract embedding of the algebraically closed characteristic-zero field k̄ into C exists here (the transcendence degrees over Q agree), or one can descend the data as usual. This yields a second proof of polyhedral type of D_gen for the actual relative image and avoids reliance on any missing generic-extension assertion.

For an integral relatively ample H on X, images of the actual lifting translations in U under b↦(t_b^*H−H) mod T lie in the projection of the relative numerical lattice along the rational subspace T. That projection is a lattice. Under the polarization isogeny, its Q-span is all U, because an isogeny is surjective on rational points after tensoring with Q (lift in a finite extension and trace). Removing finite index does not change the rational span. This constructs a discrete full lattice without a Lang–Néron assertion about the possibly very large group B(k). Identification by λ_H or dividing by a(H) in the scalar case changes it by a fixed rational homothety. Finite cosets of this translation subgroup give uniformly bounded denominators after the choices of rational origin/splitting.

**Terminological edge.** Li–Zhao v8 §2.2 explicitly says terminal fibration means the pair is terminal, immediately after giving the zero-boundary special definition for “X has terminal singularities.” Li II §2.2 spells out nonnegative boundary and log discrepancy >1 for every exceptional divisor. In total dimension at least two these conditions force every boundary coefficient <1, by blowing up a smooth codimension-two centre in its prime component. The original pair is then klt. In total dimension one, possible convention differences do not matter: the target has relative numerical space zero for a birational fibration or a single nonnegative degree ray with trivial linear image for fibration to a point. This covers the low-dimensional edge without assuming a convention about vacuous exceptional valuations.

All statements in this pass reduce the residual work to the finite-polyhedral Voronoi chamber in V/T, its boundary/faithfulness and the relative vertical lift; this journal alone does not certify the assembled strong theorem.
