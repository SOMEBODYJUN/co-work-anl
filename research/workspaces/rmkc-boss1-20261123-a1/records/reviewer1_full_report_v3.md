## Disposition of the two earlier adverse reviews

**RMKC-FULL-PROOF-REVIEW-V1.** The two-point blowup in my V1 report shows that an arbitrary support-permuting generic automorphism need not extend to the total space. V3 does not use the asserted full-group equality in arXiv:2309.04673v1 Theorem 1.3, nor the relative rational-hull theorem reached through it. Section 2, lines 57–68, instead works with the finite-index subgroup \(J'\) fixing each geometric component of the generic boundary. Its members preserve the working rational coefficients. The generic log-pluricanonical generator is multiplied by a root of unity by Fujino–Gongyo Theorem 1.1 or its klt Corollary 3.10. This scalar is in the original constant field and has order zero at every divisorial valuation. The determinant formula for absolute and relative differentials preserves that multiplier. The order of a pullback of a base Cartier divisor is unchanged by a map over the base. Hence the log-discrepancy formula in the proof is invariant. Effectivity and terminality make the valuations of log discrepancy at most one exactly the divisors on \(X\), so \(J'\) preserves these valuations in both directions, and their discrepancies recover even the vertical boundary coefficients. In particular all neutral rational points extend. This proves the finite-index input for every irregularity, including zero. The earlier example remains a counterexample only to the old intermediate equality in that preprint.

**RMKC-FULL-PROOF-REVIEW-V2.** The irrational section coefficients \(a,1-a,1/2,1/2\) on a product of projective lines give a relatively real-linearly trivial adjoint whose ordinary generic round-down has degree \(-1\) for even multiples and \(-2\) for odd multiples. Such boundaries exist in the rational numerical affine fibre by fixed-support terminal openness, with \(K_X+\Delta_0\sim_{\mathbb Q,S}0\) from the rational Hacon–Xu theorem. The original relative spaces and cones and the whole group action coincide for the two boundaries.
