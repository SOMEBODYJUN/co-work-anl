## 2. 一份源收入和全部真实末位查询

\[

\frac{t_i}{P+1}-\frac{t_i d_i}{(P+1)(P+2)}\right].\tag{D-source}
\]

\[
\sum_x\frac{d_i-q}{P+t_i+1}\ge0.\tag{D-last}

\[

u_6\ge\sum_x\frac{d_i}{\ell+1}.\tag{D-pay}
