## 3. Exact genuine-source P25 payment

Fix one outer k and one prescribed internal source i in {3,4,5}. Let

    p=sum_{j<i}ell'_j,    n=6-i,    c=(n+1)/2,    D=p+n+1.

Thus p is this SAME branch's true prefix load, not the actual-path first-four load. The six-player bounds are 0<=p<=i-1 and 1<=p+n<=5.

For any legal T,Qquery, Main's P25 theorem gives

    25 v_i' + cK >= sum m25(p,t,qquery),
    m25(p,t,qquery)=c*t+24*t/(p+n+1)+qquery/(p+1+n*t).

The genuine payment consists of 25 times source i's true IC for forcing T, each of the n-1 true intermediate successor size caps with weight1/2, the true final successor size cap with weight1, and that same branch's terminal BR against Qquery. These cap weights sum to c=(n+1)/2. The hidden successors used in that theorem are the successors of the INNER forced-T deviation. They are not frozen to the R roles from the outer branch.

With hidden inner tail bits d1,...,dn, total L=p+t+sum d and terminal z=dn, the literal cost is

    Phi25=25*(t/L)+(1/2)*sum_{j<n}d_j+z-(z/L)
                         +qquery/(p+t+sum_{j<n}d_j+1),

where a zero-load payoff is zero. Its minimum over all2^n inner tail states is exactly m25. A short analytic verification is as follows. Write s=sum_{j<n}d_j. When t=0,z=0, the difference from qquery/(p+1) is

    s/2 - qquery*s/[(p+1)(p+s+1)] >=0.

For s>=1, its bracket is nonnegative because (p+1)(p+s+1)>=2; s=0 is equality. When t=0,z=1, the cost is s/2+1+(qquery-1)/(p+s+1), which is at least qquery/(p+1), separately for qquery=0 and1. All zero inner tails attain the minimum.

When t=1, increasing a non-final tail bit changes the cost by

    1/2-(25-z)/[(p+1+s+z)(p+2+s+z)]
       -qquery/[(p+2+s)(p+3+s)] <= 1/2-24/30 <0.

The bound uses the actual six-player condition p+n<=5. With all n-1 intermediate bits equal1, changing z from0 to1 has difference

    1+24/(p+n+1)-25/(p+n)
       =[(p+n)^2-25]/[(p+n)(p+n+1)] <=0.

Thus all n inner tail bits equal1 attain m25. The final change may be zero when p+n=5, so no uniqueness or forced same-coverage claim follows merely from this minimum. Applying the resulting true nonnegative budget at a prescribed source after Q_k is valid even when Q_k was off path at source2: complete SPE quantifies over every legal ordered history.

For the frozen convention A nu <= b(z,K), one appended pure/pure row therefore has

    A(v,q,r)=m25(p,t,qquery)-25*v_i',    b=cK.

The outer average over k leaves the one right-side cK unchanged; it does not multiply or divide it by six. No old actual u_i occurs in this new row. For i=3,4,5 the exact K coefficients are respectively 2,3/2,1.

At this fixed inner true source define

    A=c+24/D,    B=1/(p+1),    C=n/[(p+1)D].

|---|---|---|
| Pure t | Pure qquery | A*t+B*qquery-C*t*qquery |
| Pure t | Omean | A*t+(o/6)*(B-C*t) |
| Omean | Pure qquery | B*qquery+(o/6)*(A-C*qquery) |

    (A+B)*d/6-C*d*(d-1)/30.
