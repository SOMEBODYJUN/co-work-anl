The deviator therefore receives `q/m+1/(b+m-i)`. The prescribed previous petal has load `t in[1,i-1]`; an unused petal exists. Maximizing `1/(m-i)-1/(t+m-i)` over all histories gives `1/(m-i)-1/(m-1)`; the repeated-petal history attains it. Root regret is zero, and the last player's fresh-petal action is a best response at all histories. Thus the all-history depth-sup norm is exactly `B=H_(m-1)-1`, independently of the core. I also checked `../new_induction_repair/factor_two_regret_attempt.md`. There are m disjoint themes: A has L=ceil((m−1)/2) unit clients and each of m−1 singleton themes has one. For a prescribed A by an earlier player, at least one A appears before the last and at least one singleton is fresh.
