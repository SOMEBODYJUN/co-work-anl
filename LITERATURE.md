# Primary-source boundaries and priority checks

Research record, 2026-09-30. This is a targeted comparison, **not** an exhaustive novelty search.

1. Simon Krogmann, Pascal Lenzner, Alexander Skopalik, Marc Uetz, Marnix C. Vos, “Equilibria in Two-Stage Facility Location with Atomic Clients,” IJCAI 2024, [official proceedings PDF](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2024/0315.pdf), [arXiv full version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03114). The official paper's Observation 4 **states** a general `k` approximate SPE upper bound. Its displayed explanation places all facilities at the location of maximum reach; the scope of that argument under disjoint facility-specific catalogs requires care. Theorem 5 gives a weighted golden-ratio lower bound, Theorem 6 hardness below that factor, and the conclusion calls tightness open. Theorem 6 does **not** prove hardness of finding a `phi` solution. Short unilateral-neighbor certificates and some pure local NE methods already appear there. Distinguish a stated bound from a new, independently proved constructive algorithm and from a matching new lower bound under heterogeneous catalogs.
2. Marnix C. Vos, 2023 thesis, [institutional PDF](https://essay.utwente.nl/96484/1/Vos_MA_EEMCS.pdf). The research drafts cite its pure customer-equilibrium repair and two-anchor ideas. Before a journal submission, inspect the precise lemma statements and bibliography against the thesis rather than relying on our summary.
3. H. W. Lenstra, “Integer Programming with a Fixed Number of Variables,” *Mathematics of Operations Research* 8(4), 1983, [author copy](https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~lenstrahw/PUBLICATIONS/1983i/art.pdf). `astra_alg/exact_algorithms.md` uses fixed-dimensional integer programming as existing machinery for the distinct-weight parameter; it does not claim to invent that algorithm.
4. Horowitz and Sahni, “Computing Partitions with Applications to the Knapsack Problem,” *JACM* 21(2), 1974, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/321812.321823). Meet-in-the-middle is prior technique; `astra_ring/mitm_solver.py` contributes a game-specific support reduction, not the general principle.

**Research interpretation, not paper fact:** `SC-PHI-v2` would close the paper's shared-catalog `phi` gap with a polynomial-bit construction; `HC-2-v1` would provide a matching heterogeneous lower bound and a particular four-seed constructive proof. Publication priority, current literature overlap and the full proof chain need independent verification before any public originality claim.
